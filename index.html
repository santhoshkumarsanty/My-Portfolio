Dimension Inspection Methodology
  • LINEAR DISTANCE
    Point-to-point measurement between two surface locations.
    1. Click first surface point
    2. Click second surface point
    3. Displays straight-line distance in mm / m
  • DIAMETER (3-POINT CIRCLE FIT)
    Fits a circumscribed circle through 3 points on a cylindrical or circular edge to compute the diameter.
    1. Click 3 points along a circular feature (bore, shaft, fillet)
    2. Algorithm computes circumscribed circle in 3D space
    3. Displays fitted circle, center marker, and ⌀ dimension
  • ANGLE (3-POINT VERTEX)
    Measures the included angle at a vertex defined by 3 surface points (A → B → C, angle at B).
    1. Click first ray point (A)
    2. Click vertex point (B)
    3. Click second ray point (C)
    4. Displays angle in degrees with visual arc
  • PITCH (TOOTH/THREAD SPACING)
    Measures the linear pitch between two adjacent gear teeth or thread crests.
    1. Click the tip of one tooth/thread crest
    2. Click the corresponding point on the next tooth/thread
    3. Displays pitch distance (P = value)
